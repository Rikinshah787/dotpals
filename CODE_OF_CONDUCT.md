# Code of Conduct

dotpals is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to help keep it that way.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in dotpals a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to coding agents or to open source.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks this code, tell the maintainer privately through GitHub: use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/rikinshah787/dotpals/security/advisories/new), or message [@Rikinshah787](https://github.com/Rikinshah787). Every report is read and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone who keeps breaking it.
